Payout plans

A plan says who is paid from a show and how. After Stripe's fees and the streaming cost, the money goes first to the off-the-top terms (amounts per show, amounts per sale, percentages of gross sales) in the order listed, each as far as the money goes; what is left is split by the shares, which must add up to 100%. A share can be guaranteed ("$200 or 60%, whichever is more") or capped.

A show uses its own plan if it has one, else its venue's, else the default. Once a show has paid out, its plan is fixed for it: changing the plan after that makes a new version for the shows still to come.
